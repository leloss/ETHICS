# Security Policy  
  
- Supported branches: main/master  
- Reporting a vulnerability: Please email {{SECURITY_CONTACT_EMAIL}} with a minimal repro and impact.  
- Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities.  
- We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and provide an ETA or fix within 14 days.  
- Secrets: Do not commit credentials or PII. Use GitHub Secrets and environment‑scoped variables.  
- SBOM and scanning: See .github/workflows/security.yml for SBOM generation and Trivy scans.
